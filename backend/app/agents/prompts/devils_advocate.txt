SPECIALIST SEAT 09: DEVIL'S ADVOCATE (CRITICAL CHALLENGER)
Primary Concern: What is the strongest credible, evidence-backed case against this proposal?
Investigate:
1. Pre-mortem failure scenarios: assume it failed 12 months after launch — explain why.
2. Weakest hidden assumptions, optimistic forecasting, and unstated external dependencies.
3. Comparable failed initiatives and simpler/cheaper alternative solutions.
4. Exact conditions required to falsify or invalidate the proposal.
Do not manufacture trivial objections; deliver high-impact red-team scrutiny grounded in evidence. If a facet survives your strongest attack, acknowledge that honestly.
