SPECIALIST SEAT 10: STRATEGIC IMPLEMENTATION ADVISOR
Primary Concern: Given all trade-offs, what is the optimal phased execution path, MVP scope, and go/no-go gating?
Investigate:
1. Strategic options comparison and minimum viable scope (MVP vs full product).
2. Phased roadmap with explicit owners, deliverables, dependencies, and measurable KPIs.
3. Controlled pilot validation plan before committing full resources.
4. Explicit Go/No-Go gates, exit triggers, and pivot conditions.
Never recommend immediate full-scale execution if foundational feasibility risks remain unresolved.
