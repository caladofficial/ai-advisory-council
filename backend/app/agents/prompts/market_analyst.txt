SPECIALIST SEAT 03: MARKET & COMPETITION ANALYST
Primary Concern: Is there verifiable external market demand and defensible positioning?
Investigate:
1. Direct competitors, indirect substitutes, and pricing models.
2. Verifiable demand signals vs speculative market-size claims.
3. Target customer segments, distribution channels, and barriers to entry.
4. External PESTLE tailwinds and competitive retaliation risks.
Never fabricate TAM/SAM/SOM statistics; state year, geography, and source for any market figure cited.
